# Sharpsville Youth Baseball Association (SYBA) Portal

Welcome to the official management portal for the **Sharpsville Youth Baseball Association**. This platform is designed to streamline registration, team communication, and league safety for parents, coaches, and administrators.

## 🚀 Getting Started (For Testers)

If you are reviewing this portal for the first time, follow these steps to see the full features in action:

1.  **Sign Up**: Create an account and select the **Admin** role (for testing purposes).
2.  **Initialize Data**: Go to the **Admin Dashboard** and click on **"Seed POC Data"**. This instantly creates a sample "Spring 2024" season with teams, coaches, and players.
3.  **Explore Roles**: You can now log in or switch roles to see how the app looks for different users.

---

## 👥 User Roles & Features

### 🏠 For Parents
Manage your family's baseball season from one dashboard.
*   **Player Registration**: Add your children to the system and enroll them in active seasons.
*   **Safety First**: Securely upload birth certificates for age verification. Once verified by the league, these sensitive files are automatically deleted to protect your privacy.
*   **Team Directory**: View your child's team roster and coordinate with other parents (carpools, snacks) based on shared privacy settings.
*   **Schedules & RSVPs**: View practice and game times and let the coach know if your child will attend.

### ⚾ For Coaches
Lead your team with better tools and communication.
*   **Team Roster**: Access player names, jersey numbers, and parent contact info instantly.
*   **Medical Alerts**: See high-visibility alerts for players with allergies or medical needs.
*   **AI Practice Builder**: Use built-in AI to generate customized practice drills based on your team's age group and skill level.
*   **Communication Hub**: Send urgent "Broadcast" messages (like rainout alerts) directly to your team's parents.
*   **Compliance**: Upload and track your required state background checks (PIAA) to ensure you are cleared to coach.

### 🛠️ For Administrators
The "Command Center" for running the association.
*   **Master Roster**: A bird's-eye view of every player in the league. Assign "Unassigned" players to teams with a single click.
*   **Compliance Audit**: Review and approve volunteer background checks. Track who is "Cleared to Coach" and send bulk reminders for missing documents.
*   **Season Management**: Create new seasons, define age divisions (T-Ball, Minors, etc.), and set registration fees.
*   **Secure Redaction**: Review player birth certificates and "Verify & Redact" them in one step to minimize the league's data liability.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security
We take your family's data seriously.
*   **Automatic Redaction**: Sensitive identity documents are deleted immediately after verification.
*   **Parent Privacy Toggle**: Parents choose whether to share their phone number or email with other families on their team.
*   **Role-Based Access**: Information is only shown to those who need it (e.g., Coaches see medical notes; Admins see background checks).
