**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Lens:** a non-technical parent on a phone (390×844) registering a child for
the season. Findings come from the Phase 1 test suite, code review, and a
mobile screenshot pass of every critical screen (script:

**Priorities:** Critical = blocks trust or task completion · High =

## 1. Getting into registration (home → signup → form)

### 1.1 ⛔ CRITICAL — "Register Player" loses brand-new parents

- **Location:** `src/firebase/sport-context.tsx` (SportProvider gate, ~line 167)
  + `src/app/(auth)/signup/page.tsx` / `login/page.tsx`
- **Current:** Home → "Register Player" → login → "Create an account" →
  signup with `?redirect=/parent/enroll`. The moment the account is created,
  the SportProvider gate sees "logged-in user, no sport selected yet" while
  still on `/signup`, renders `RedirectToHome`, and pushes the parent to `/`
  → `/parent/dashboard`. The redirect to the registration form is lost
  (reproduced 100% in the E2E suite; kept as `test.fixme`).
- **Problem:** the single most important first-time journey strands the
  parent on a dashboard that says "Welcome back" to someone who has never
  been here, with their actual task (register a child) demoted to a small
  "Enroll Now" card they must discover.
- **Proposed fix:** exclude the auth pages from the gate, exactly like
  `/parent/enroll` already is:
  `!pathname.startsWith('/parent/enroll') && !pathname.startsWith('/signup') && !pathname.startsWith('/login')`.
  Those pages already write the sport and route the user themselves.
  Acceptance: E2E `test.fixme('signup with redirect lands directly on the
  enrollment form')` un-fixme'd and passing.
- **Priority:** Critical

### 1.2 HIGH — Primary CTA is "Sign In", not "Register Player"

- **Location:** `src/app/page.tsx` (~line 252)
- **Current:** "Sign In" is the filled blue button; "Register Player" is the
  outline secondary next to it.
- **Problem:** on day one nearly every visitor's job is registration, and the
  eye lands on the filled button. Returning users will find Sign In anyway.
- **Proposed fix:** swap the variants — "Register Player" filled primary,
  "Sign In" outline. Keep order: Register Player first (left), Sign In second.
- **Priority:** High

### 1.3 HIGH — "Registration open" message is the smallest text on the page

- **Location:** `src/app/page.tsx` hero eyebrow (~line 238)
- **Current:** `REGISTRATION OPEN FOR SPRING 2026 — CLOSES …` in ~11px
  uppercase letter-spaced text above the title.
- **Problem:** it reads as decoration. Parents skim right past the one
  seasonal fact that should trigger action (and its deadline).
- **Proposed fix:** render it as a banner card under the CTA buttons:
  rounded-xl, light primary background (`bg-primary/10`), normal sentence
  case, 14–16px: "✅ Registration is open for Spring 2026 — closes June 30."
  Keep the closed-state variant as is.
- **Priority:** High

### 1.4 LOW — Auth copy is system-speak

- **Location:** `src/app/(auth)/login/page.tsx`, `signup/page.tsx`
- **Current:** "Portal Login" / "Enter your credentials to access your
  dashboard"; "Create SYBA Account" / "Sign up to join the association".
- **Problem:** "credentials", "portal", "association" are institution words,
  not parent words.
- **Proposed fix:** Login: title "Welcome back", description "Sign in to
  manage your player's season." Signup: title "Create your account",
  description "Register your kids, pay online, and get schedules — all in
  one place."
- **Priority:** Low

## 2. The enrollment stepper (`src/app/parent/enroll/EnrollmentStepper.tsx`)

### 2.1 ⛔ CRITICAL — On phones, form fields scroll underneath the blue header inside a box-in-a-box

- **Location:** EnrollmentStepper card (~line 956):
  `max-h-[calc(100dvh-280px)] md:max-h-none` + `CardContent … overflow-y-auto`
- **Current:** on mobile the card caps its own height and scrolls its
  contents internally. Screenshot evidence: on step 1 the First/Last-name
  inputs are half-swallowed under the blue "Season & Division" header; on
  step 2 the shirt-size select is clipped at the top. The page also scrolls,
  so there are two competing scroll areas.
- **Problem:** fields visually disappear under the header mid-entry; parents
  on phones lose track of what's above/below, and nothing signals that the
  *card* (not the page) must be scrolled.
- **Proposed fix:** remove the internal scroll on mobile — drop the
  `max-h-…` and `overflow-y-auto`/`min-h-0` so the card grows naturally and
  the page is the only scroll container. To keep Back/Next always reachable
  (the likely reason the cap was added), make the CardFooter
  `sticky bottom-0` (it already has `bg-card border-t shadow-sm`), sitting
  above the bottom tab bar.
- **Priority:** Critical

### 2.2 ⛔ CRITICAL — "Next" goes gray with no explanation (worst on step 2)

- **Location:** Next button `disabled={…}` expression (~line 1469)
- **Current:** one combined boolean disables Next. On step 2 the most common
  blocker — the **required birth certificate** — lives at the very bottom of
  the step, below optional fields, off-screen on a phone. The parent fills
  the visible fields, sees a gray Next, and gets no hint why.
- **Problem:** dead-end with zero feedback; this is exactly where a
  non-technical parent gives up or calls the league.
- **Proposed fix:** keep the validation but explain it. Under the footer,
  when Next is disabled, render one amber line naming the *first* missing
  item, e.g. "To continue, upload {player}'s birth certificate (below)" /
  "Add at least one emergency contact (name, phone, relationship)" /
  football: "Enter a weight estimate". Derive from the same conditions the
  `disabled` already checks, so they can't drift apart.
- **Priority:** Critical

### 2.3 ⛔ CRITICAL — A parent can register and pay for the same child twice

- **Location:** `checkDuplicate()` (~line 336)
- **Current:** the duplicate check queries
  `collectionGroup('enrollments').where(playerId).where(seasonId)`. Firestore
  rules only allow enrollment reads provably scoped to
  `parentUserId == auth.uid`, so the query is **always permission-denied**;
  the `catch` swallows it and returns `'none'`. Verified by the E2E suite:
  a parent with a paid enrollment sails through to payment again. The
  in-stepper "Resume Payment" banner is unreachable for the same reason.
- **Problem:** double charges = refund work for the board and instant trust
  damage with families.
- **Proposed fix:** a parent only ever needs *their own* enrollments — query
  the subcollection directly (no collectionGroup, no new index, rules
  already allow it):
  `query(collection(db,'userProfiles',user.uid,'enrollments'), where('playerId','==',…), where('seasonId','==',…))`.
  Also stop returning `'none'` on error — surface a toast ("We couldn't
  verify this registration — please try again") and keep the user on step 1.
  Acceptance: un-fixme the E2E duplicate test.
- **Priority:** Critical

### 2.4 HIGH — Step circles have no names on phones

- **Location:** step indicator (~line 936), labels are `hidden sm:inline`
- **Current:** mobile shows ① › ② › ③ with no words; the only context is the
  card header after scrolling.
- **Problem:** parents can't see where they are or what's left ("is 3 the
  payment?").
- **Proposed fix:** on mobile, show a single line under the circles:
  "Step {n} of 3 · {label}" (labels already exist via `getStepLabel`).
  Desktop unchanged.
- **Priority:** High

### 2.5 HIGH — The only *required* item on step 2 is listed last

- **Location:** step 2 layout (~lines 1158–1374)
- **Current:** order is Uniform # (optional) → Shirt size (optional) →
  Medical notes (optional) → Emergency contact (required) → Documents
  (birth certificate **required**, physical optional).
- **Problem:** required things below optional things, and below the fold —
  the cause of the gray-Next dead-end in 2.2.
- **Proposed fix:** reorder: Emergency contact → Documents (with a red
  "Required" badge on Birth Certificate, mirroring the `*` convention) →
  then the optional uniform/shirt/medical block under a header "Optional —
  helps us prepare".
- **Priority:** High

### 2.6 LOW — "League age" hint is insider jargon

- **Location:** league-age banner (~line 1064)
- **Current:** "League age for 2026-05-01: **8** (divisions filtered to best match)"
- **Problem:** raw ISO date and the term "league age" mean nothing to a
  first-year parent.
- **Proposed fix:** "For this season, {first name} plays as an
  **8-year-old** (their age on May 1, 2026). We're showing the divisions
  that fit."
- **Priority:** Low

### 2.7 LOW — "Add Player" button label is ambiguous

- **Location:** review-step footer (~line 1494)
- **Current:** "Add Player" next to "Proceed to Payment".
- **Problem:** could read as "add this player". It actually starts a second
  child's registration (sibling discount!).
- **Proposed fix:** rename to "+ Register Another Child" and add a one-line
  note under the fee box: "Brothers & sisters: $50 each after the first
  player." (pricing from `calculateCartPricing`).
- **Priority:** Low

## 3. Payment handoff (Stripe)

### 3.1 HIGH — Backing out of Stripe dumps the parent on a blank form

- **Location:** `src/app/api/stripe/checkout/route.ts` (~line 162,
  `cancel_url: ${baseUrl}/parent/enroll`)
- **Current:** tapping Stripe's "Back" returns to `/parent/enroll` — a fresh
  empty step 1. The enrollment they just built exists (status
  `pending_payment`) but the stepper gives no sign of it (and its resume
  banner is dead per 2.3).
- **Problem:** looks like everything was lost; parents will re-enter the
  child from scratch (and, with 2.3 unfixed, possibly pay twice).
- **Proposed fix:** point `cancel_url` to `/parent/dashboard`, which already
  shows the amber "Payment Due — {player} — Pay $X →" card (E2E-tested).
  One-line change, no new UI.
- **Priority:** High

### 3.2 HIGH — Stripe page says "New business sandbox"

- **Location:** Stripe account settings (not code)
- **Current:** the hosted checkout header shows the placeholder business
  name; statement descriptor unset.
- **Problem:** a parent about to type a card number sees a business name
  that isn't the league — the #1 "is this a scam?" trigger.
- **Proposed fix:** before launch, set Stripe public business name to
  "Sharpsville Youth Baseball Association", upload the logo/brand color in
  Stripe Branding, and set the statement descriptor (e.g. "SYBA BASEBALL").
  Already on the launch checklist; elevating because it's customer-facing.
- **Priority:** High

## 4. Confirmation (`src/app/parent/enroll/success/page.tsx`)

### 4.1 HIGH — "Registration Complete!" doesn't say what happens next

- **Location:** authenticated success card (~line 292)
- **Current:** checkmark, "Your payment was received and your player has
  been enrolled", "confirmation email on its way", Back to Dashboard.
- **Problem:** the parent's real questions start here: When do I hear about
  teams? Is the birth certificate okay? Do I need to do anything else? With
  no answers they email the board.
- **Proposed fix:** add a left-aligned "What happens next" list to the card:
  1. "We'll review {player}'s documents — you'll get an email when verified."
  2. "Team assignments are announced after evaluations — watch Announcements."
  3. "Game and practice schedules will appear in your Schedule tab."
  (Plus the physical-form reminder when `physicalUrl` is missing — the
  dashboard prompt already exists, repeat it here.)
- **Priority:** High

### 4.2 LOW — Raw enrollment ID on the success screen

- **Location:** same card (~line 307)
- **Current:** "Enrollment ID: 6f1c2e7a-…" (comma-joined UUIDs for carts).
- **Problem:** a 36-character code reads as something to write down; it's
  only useful for support.
- **Proposed fix:** remove it, or collapse to "Reference for support:
  {first 8 chars}" in 11px muted text.
- **Priority:** Low

## 5. Mobile ergonomics (global)

### 5.1 HIGH — Tap targets below the 44px minimum across all key screens

- **Location:** `src/components/ui/input.tsx`, `select.tsx` (`h-10` = 40px);
  `button.tsx` size `sm` (36px); small pills in EnrollmentStepper
  ("Add new child" `h-7` = 28px, "Review Cart" `h-7`), sidebar "Collapse"
  (32px).
- **Current:** measured by the Phase 1 audit at 360/390/768 widths — every
  text input and select trigger is 40px; several controls 28–36px. (Primary
  CTAs are already 44–48px — good.)
- **Problem:** mis-taps on the exact fields a parent must fill; Apple/Google
  both specify 44px minimums.
- **Proposed fix:** raise base `Input` and `SelectTrigger` to `h-11` (44px)
  globally (desktop tolerates it well; inputs already use 16px text on
  mobile, which correctly prevents iOS auto-zoom). Bump the stepper's pill
  buttons to `h-9`+ with larger padding. Then re-enable the
  `touch-target audit` E2E test (currently `fixme`) as the regression guard.
- **Priority:** High

### 5.2 Already good — for the record

- Bottom tab bar (Home/Schedule/Family/Inbox) for parents on mobile is the
  right pattern; 44px hamburger; notification dots.
- Friendly mapped auth errors ("Invalid email or password…"), enumeration-safe
  password reset, loading spinners/skeletons everywhere, top-positioned
  mobile toasts, error boundary for permission errors.
- Dashboard empty/pending/paid states are clear and actionable (E2E-tested).
